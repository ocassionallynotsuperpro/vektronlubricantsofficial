SAE 5W-30

Vektron Premium Fully Synthetic Engine Oil

VEKTRON LUBRICANT PREMIUM FULLY SYNTHETIC 5W-30 API SP is a fully synthetic engine oil tailored for smoother acceleration and provides outstanding engine wear protection even under the most demanding driving conditions.

To meet the demands of today's most advanced vehicles — including downsized turbo engines prone to damage caused by LSPI — VEKTRON LUBRICANT PREMIUM fully synthetic products have already been tested and approved to both API SP and ILSAC GF-6 specifications.
