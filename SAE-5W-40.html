SAE 5W-40 C3

Vektron Premium Fully Synthetic PAO Engine Oil

VEKTRON SAE 5W-40 C3 GASOLINE/DIESEL is designed from premium fully synthetic base oil, used in passenger cars equipped with gasoline, diesel, and LPG engines.

This is formulated to meet the engine oil requirement of European automobiles makers such as Volkswagen, BMW and Benz.

Performance Standards

ACEA:C3-16
API: SP, ILSAC GF-6A
OEM BMW Long Life-04
OEM Mercedes-Benz:MB229.51/229.52
OEM Volkswagen:VW502.00/505.00/505.01

Features & Benefits

This low SAPS (Sulfated Ash, Phosphrus and Sylfur) engine oil provide long life for exhaust after treatment devices including diesel partulate filter
Synthetic oil flow in cold condition assures easy start and good drivabillity and prevent LPSI.
High viscosity index ensures adequate oil film strength at hot operating condition.
VEKTRON SAE 5W-40 C3 GASOLINE/DIESEL'S excellent clean up properties help to remove dirt and sludge and vanish building up.
